# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Anyone who needs a quick rule-of-three (regra de 3) calculation — students doing homework, professionals converting proportions at work, or everyday users solving proportion problems on the fly. Context: they arrive with a specific problem, want the answer fast, and leave. Mobile and desktop, often in a hurry.

## Product Purpose

A real-time rule-of-three calculator that makes proportion math instant and frictionless. Success = the user gets their answer in under 5 seconds, with enough confidence to act on it. Supports both direct and inverse proportions, adjustable decimal precision, calculation history, and clipboard copy.

## Brand Personality

**Snappy, polished, approachable.** The speed of a native tool with the personality of a well-crafted product. Not sterile — it should feel good to use, with subtle moments of delight. Not loud — the math is the star, design supports it.

## Anti-references

- Generic calculator apps with grid-of-buttons layouts
- Enterprise dashboards or SaaS product aesthetics
- Educational platforms with childish palettes or mascots
- Cookie-cutter DaisyUI default themes

## Design Principles

1. **Instant clarity** — The relationship between inputs and output must be immediately obvious. No guessing which field is which or where the result lives.
2. **Speed is the feature** — Every interaction should feel faster than thinking about it. Zero friction between "I have a problem" and "I have the answer."
3. **Confidence through craft** — Precise typography, intentional motion, and polished details signal that the math behind the interface is equally reliable.
4. **Personality without noise** — Subtle character in motion, color, and copy. Never decorative for decoration's sake.
5. **One screen, no maze** — Everything lives on one focused surface. No navigation, no routing, no hidden panels. The constraint is a feature.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance: 4.5:1 contrast ratio for body text, 3:1 for large text and UI components
- Visible focus indicators on all interactive elements
- Full keyboard navigation (Tab through fields, Enter to advance)
- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected for all animations
- Semantic HTML and ARIA labels in Portuguese
- Number inputs accessible to screen readers with clear field identification
